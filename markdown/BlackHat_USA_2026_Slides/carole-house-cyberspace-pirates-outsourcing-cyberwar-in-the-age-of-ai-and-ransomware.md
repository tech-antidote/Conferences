## Slide 13

## ALL HANDS ON DECK: THE ARGUMENT FOR LETTERS OF MARQUE

- **_Massive Scale Offset:_** Deputizes elite private capability against 50:1 adversary margins

- **_Formalizing Gray Zones:_** Can move existing/ongoing private disruptions into structured accountability; acknowledges reality of USG capabilities and America’s need for defense

- **_Imposing Economic Costs:_** Asset seizures force compellence where indictments and sanctions aren’t achieving impact

- **_Constitutional Precedent:_** Leverages explicit Article I authorities to adapt to the realities of asymmetric warfare

## Slide 16

## THE HORIZON IS CLOSER THAN IT LOOKS – AND THE LAW STILL SAYS NO – SO WHERE TO NOW?

Privateers exist today, in gray zones.

The actual decision: The actual decision: formalize and regulate, accepting responsibility and building governance - or accept the costs of the status quo.

The Three Gaps test – does it solve Authority, Capacity, and Governance? (current bills address the first two only)

Democracies are unlikely to avoid the delegation question as cyber operations become increasingly automated and economically asymmetric.

##### **THREE THINGS ANY ANSWER MUST DO:**

Keep authority where accountability sits

Structure what the private sector is already doing informally

Pass the five governance tests before you call it a solution
