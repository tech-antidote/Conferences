## Slide 3

### THE CORE ARGUMENT

Cybercrime networks – including ransomware transnational criminal organizations (TCOs), pig-butchering scam compounds – are not mere criminals to be prosecuted. They are transnational organized crime ecosystems with state-level consequence-generation capacity, requiring campaign planning logic and lessons from asymmetric warfare to be applied.

Military campaign planning doctrine provides specific analytic tools for the problems counter-cybercrime police most consistently fails to solve.

The U.S. accelerated a genuine paradigm shift in 2021, applying whole-of-government campaign thinking to ransomware, which produced some real and some ephemeral results. A doctrinal lens can help us evaluate what the next evolution requires.

Disruption operations are not offensive adjuncts to a defensive strategy. They are the precondition for defense to compound.

## Slide 18

### APPLYING THE FRAMEWORK TO A CYBER TCO

**Centers of Gravity (COGs)**

- Priority Networks based on Impact, Reach
- Common Actors across Networks
- Permissive Haven Jurisdiction & Enabler Infrastructure (political protection, regulatory arbitrage, unverified digital/telecom rails)

**Critical Vulnerabilities (Root Causes)**

- Reporting Gaps and Silos
- Digital Identity & Telco Spoofing
- Crypto Off-Ramps and Shadow Banking
- Jurisdictional Arbitrage & Policy Gaps

**LOE1: SHAPE & DENY – DRIVE UPSTREAM HARDENING**

Fraud register and playbooks; reporting hub harmonization/integrations; digital identity and VoIP transit standards; consumer awareness campaigns; informed consent & friction architectures; fraud info sharing channels; domain & cloud registrar KYC* (?)

**LOE2: DISRUPT & DEGRADE – SYNC ACTIVE DISRUPTION WITH PUBLIC-PRIVATE CAPABILITIES**

Increased operational tempo cross-sector takedowns; expand PPPs for rapid tipping and queueing and disruption; ad-tech & search engine fraud takedowns; cross-sector fraud playbooks; fraud data schemas

**LOE3: FINANCIAL INTERDICTION – RECAPITALIZE AND SCALE COUNTER-ILLICI TFINANCE**

Expand and automate Financial Fraud Kill Chain (FFKC)/Rapid Response Program (RRP); reconstitute and expand crypto enforcement; scale civil, sanctions, and criminal enforcement

**LOE4: INTERNATIONAL RESILIENCE – REENGAGE INT'L COALITIONS & BUILD LASTING ACCOUNTABILITY**

Re-engage/re-fund int'l capacity-building orgs (e.g. Interpol, FATF, Egmont) and partner jurisdictions; Deploy (multi-lateral) diplomatic, trade, and regulatory leverage to squeeze haven jurisdictions

**Effects**

- Systemic Visibility & Prevention – Real-time data sharing; Attempts/incidents prevented
- Operational Friction – Spoofing cost & disruption rate; reduction in adversarial optempo
- Financial Impact – Time-to-freeze & capital cost; reduction in payments and funds laundered
- Victim & Coalition Output – Redress velocity & haven action
