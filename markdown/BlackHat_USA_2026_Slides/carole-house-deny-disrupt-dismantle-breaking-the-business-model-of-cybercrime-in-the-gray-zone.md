## Slide 3

### THE CORE ARGUMENT

Cybercrime networks – including ransomware transnational criminal organizations (TCOs), pig-butchering scam compounds – are not mere criminals to be prosecuted. They are transnational organized crime ecosystems with **01** state-level consequence-generation capacity, requiring campaign planning logic and lessons from asymmetric warfare to be applied.

Military campaign planning doctrine provides specific analytic tools for the problems counter-cybercrime police **01** most consistently fails to solve.

The U.S. accelerated a genuine paradigm shift in 2021, applying whole-of-government campaign thinking to ransomware, which produced some real and some ephemeral results. A doctrinal lens can help us evaluate what **01** the next evolution requires.

Disruption operations are not offensive adjuncts to a defensive strategy. They are the precondition for defense to **01** compound.
