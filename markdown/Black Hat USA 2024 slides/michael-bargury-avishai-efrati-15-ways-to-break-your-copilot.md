- The result: Sensitive data over-sharing and leakage.

## Slide 55

###### Potential problem: User input

- The action inputs can be determined dynamically.

- By **default** , the copilot tries to determine the relevant _environment_ and _data table_ , based on user input.

- User input is the mother of all exploitation origin points.

- The result is twofold:

   - Wrong analysis by the copilot will lead to out-of-scope access.

   - • Malicious users can deliberately prompt-inject other .

   - environments and data, and gain unintended data access
