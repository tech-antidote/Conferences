## Slide 4

### Other Important Controls

**Incident Response Plan & Test**

**Patch and Vuln Mgmt.**

Scan monthly (min)

Define processes

Define Action Plans and Mitigate

Clear RACI

Monitor, esp. Crisis mgmt. & KEVs TTX

**Awareness Training & Phish Testing**

Mandatory annual training (min)

Phishing Simulation monthly

Report & Response SOC playbook

**Asset Hardening & Restriction**

Baseline config assets (endpoint, server, network)

Restrict inbound Internet ports (RDP, SMB, SSH, DB)

**Logging & Network Protection**

Catalog assets for logging

Integrate with SIEM SOC Playbooks (e.g., Domain Admin use)

**3**<sup>**rd**</sup> **Party Risk EOL Systems Management** Determine critical Segment EOL vendors systems

Focus on key controls (this list) Build 3<sup>rd</sup> party Lifecycle Program

Invest in upgrading out of EOL, if feasible
