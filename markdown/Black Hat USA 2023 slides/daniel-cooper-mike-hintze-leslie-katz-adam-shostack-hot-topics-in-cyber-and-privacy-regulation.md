## Slide 4

### A Dramatic Year for U.S. Privacy Law

The past year has seen some major developments in U.S. privacy law that will be enormously impactful on a wide range of organization. From a growing number of state "comprehensive" privacy laws to new rules for "health data," this talk with highlight some key developments and important takeaways.
