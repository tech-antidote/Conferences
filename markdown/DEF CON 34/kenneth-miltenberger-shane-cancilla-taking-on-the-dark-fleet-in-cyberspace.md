**UNCLASSIFIED**

### Navigation Systems Tampering – Long Range Identification & Tracking

- LRIT: Vessels over 300 gross tons are mandated by the International Maritime Organization (IMO) to transmit their identity & location every 6 hrs to one of 4 bodies:

   - Coastal State

   - Flag State

   - Port State

   - SAR Authorities

- Typically built on INMARSAT-C

   - Closed, encrypted, private communications for coastal and flag state monitoring.

- Spoofing from a legal & safety prospective is much more serious, but typically harder in practice.

- To spoof, simulate external GPS or tamper with firmware
