## Slide 12

11 Tips for Counterfeiting Event Credentials

● What do you need to get into? ● Are there better ways in?

● How are credentials checked? ● Countersurveillance

## Slide 13

Tip #1 - Look the Part

● Play dress up

● Bring props

● Match attitude & demeanor

## Slide 14

Tip #2 - Retouch & Recreate

● Photocopies stand out

● Matched level of detail is better than element accuracy

● Clear & clean prints

## Slide 15

## Tip #3 - Touch Test

● Fingers are nearly as important as eyes

● Determine when credentials might be handled and how

## Slide 16

Tip #4 - Stay in Your Lane

● You dressed for a part, don’t act out of it.

● Becoming someone’s problem is the quick way to getting in worse trouble.

## Slide 17

Tip #5 - Accessorize!

● Ensure you have everything associated with a valid credential ● Inventory items that accompany properly issued credentials

● Receipts, stickers, etc

## Slide 18

## Tip #6 - Colors Matter

● Color errors stand out more than detail mistakes

● Use inconspicuous items like clothing or pamphlets as color references in photos

## Slide 19

Tip #7 - Consider Material (Laminates aren’t pouches)

● Pouches versus laminates

● Thermal paper versus printer paper ● Staple, clasp, etc color

## Slide 20

Tip #8 - Consider Process (Cutting isn’t tearing)

● Cut versus torn

● Stamped versus printed ● Surface printed versus dyed cloth ● Relief versus intaglio

## Slide 21

Tip #9 - Match photo quality

● Is there a consistent background? ● Photos often printed separately ● Downgrade quality if needed

## Slide 22

Tip #10 - Scope Serial Numbers

● Research required

● Depending on your plan, passing but invalid might be ok

● Sometimes invalid might be better than already used

## Slide 23

Tip #11 - Age & Condition

● Day 1 versus Day 11 ● Multi-event credentials

● Credentials issued ahead of the event
