## Slide 3

What is Phrack?

Zine founded in 1985

Older than the World Wide Web

Originally covering phone phreaking, Phrack later expanded to discuss computer security

Influential papers include The Hacker Manifesto (7:3), Smashing The Stack For Fun And Profit (49:14), The Art Of Port Scanning (51:11)

## Slide 4

# Phrack Eras

1985 - Taran King and Knight Lightning

- It all began on the Metal Shop BBS run by Taran King

- Eventually raided in 1989 by US Secret Service for disclosure of E911 documents

- - This led to the creation of the EFF

- The Hacker Crackdown - Bruce Sterling

- The paper you all know: “The Conscience of a Hacker” / Hacker Manifesto (The Mentor)
