## Slide 22

# CHALLENGES AND TAKEAWAYS

### Local Constraints

**_Challenge:_** Budgetary limitations lead to traditional plant operations being prioritized over cyber

#### **_Takeaway:_**

We can’t solve this without experts to help “bridge the gap”

### Capacity Limits

**_Challenge:_**

Given little bandwidth for cyber, systems cannot implement free resources

#### **_Takeaway:_**

We need resources <u>and</u> people to deploy and manage them

DEF CON > Feds

#### **_Challenge:_**

Federal support is notoriously slow, and even less likely with this Administration

#### **_Takeaway:_**

Again, this community & philanthropy will be the ones to step up

Unique Systems

#### **_Challenge:_**

Water is extremely trust-based, and it takes time to establish connections

#### **_Takeaway:_**

We need trusted partners on the ground to solve this at scale

_We need a nationwide cyber model for water systems…_
