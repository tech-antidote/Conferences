## Slide 13

## **Ships transport 90% of global trade**

**Maritime transport contributes $5.4T annually to U.S. GDP** Maritime trade is vital to the U.S. economic and national security.

**Maritime 5.0 = ‘Always Connected’ and ‘Fully Digital’** The drive for efficiency, sustainability, and technological adoption is rapidly changing the maritime industry and creating new business models and new vulnerabilities.

**Maritime cybersecurity is not keeping pace**

Complex international framework, diversity of equipment suppliers, and lack of risk awareness has limited the adoption of maritime cybersecurity measures.
